Why sell on Farmkeke

Grow your sales without the middlemen

More buyers

Guaranteed payment

Delivery handled

How selling works

From new order to money in your bank

1
Receive an order

You get an alert by push notification and SMS. The buyer has already paid.

2
Accept within 24 hours

If you don't accept in time, the buyer gets a full refund.

3
Pack the order

Mark it as packed and a Farmkeke rider is sent to collect it.

4
Rider delivers

The buyer receives it at home, or collects it from a pickup point.

5
Get paid

Once the buyer confirms, the money goes to your bank account, minus the Farmkeke commission.

What you'll need to apply

Take clear photos with your phone. Our team checks every application.

Government ID
NIN slip, voter's card, driver's licence or international passport
Farm photos
At least three photos of your farm and produce
Proof of address
A recent utility bill or a letter from your community
Farmers association letter
A letter from your local farmers association or cooperative
Bank details for payouts
An account in your name or your registered farm name

Simple, fair pricing

Joining and listing are free. Farmkeke takes a small commission only on completed sales. You'll see the exact rate before you submit your application.

Farmer questions

Before you apply

Still unsure? Talk to our farmer support team.

Contact farmer support
How long does approval take?

Most applications are reviewed within 3 working days. We may call you or visit your farm before approving.

Can I sell if I farm on a small plot?

Yes. Farmkeke is for farmers of all sizes, as long as you grow what you sell and pass our checks.

When do I get paid?

As soon as the buyer confirms they received the order. The money is sent to the bank account you registered.

What if a buyer reports a problem?

You'll get an alert and have 12 hours to reply with your side. Farmkeke then decides the outcome, usually within a day.

Can I manage my farm on the website?

Not yet. Farmers apply, list produce and manage orders in the Farmkeke app.
