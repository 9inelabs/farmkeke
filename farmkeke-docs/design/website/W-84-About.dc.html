About Farmkeke

We're shortening the road from farm to home

Farmkeke started in Owerri with a simple idea: the people who grow our food should earn more, and the families who buy it should pay less. Today we connect households with verified farmers across Imo State.

Our mission
Fair prices for families. Fair pay for farmers. Fresh food for everyone.
Our story

Too many hands between the farm and your table

In Nigeria, produce often passes through several traders before it reaches a household. Each one adds a markup, and farmers see only a small part of what families pay.

Farmkeke lets families buy directly from farmers. We check every farm, hold every payment safely until the order arrives, and run our own riders, so both sides can trust each other.

We're starting in Owerri and growing one community at a time.

What we stand for

Our values

Fairness

Farmers set their own prices. Families pay what the food is worth, not the cost of every middleman.

Trust

Verified farms, protected payments and honest reviews. No surprises.

Community

We hire local riders and support local farmers associations and cooperatives.

How we check farmers

Every farm is verified before it sells

Our team reviews each application by hand, and we keep checking after approval.

1
Identity and address

Government ID and proof of address are checked.

2
Farm and association

Farm photos and a farmers association letter confirm they grow what they sell.

3
Call or visit

We may call the farmer or visit the farm before approving.

4
Ongoing reviews

Ratings and problem reports help us keep standards high.
