What is Farmkeke

A fairer way to buy fresh food

Produce in Nigeria passes through many hands before it reaches you. Farmkeke takes out the middlemen, so you pay less and farmers earn more.

Fair prices

Buy straight from the people who grow your food, without market markups along the way.

Verified farmers

Every farm sends its ID, farm photos and association letter, and is approved by our team before it can sell.

Protected payments

Your money is held by Farmkeke and released to the farmer only after you confirm your order arrived.

How it works
