How it works

Fresh food from the farm, in four steps

Whether you shop in the app or on the website, every Farmkeke order works the same way.

Start shoppingHow payments are protected
1

Choose your produce

Tell us your area, then browse fresh produce from verified farms that deliver near you.

  • Shop eight categories, from vegetables to meat and fish
  • See the farm, price and pack size for every item
  • Order from several farms in one checkout
2

Pay securely

Pay with card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack. Farmkeke holds the money safely.

  • No account needed. Check out as a guest
  • Each farm gets its own order, and has 24 hours to accept
  • If a farm doesn't accept, you get a full refund
3

Receive your order

A Farmkeke rider brings it to your door, or you collect it from a pickup point.

  • Follow each step: accepted, packed, on the way
  • Chat with the farmer about your order
  • Pickup orders wait for you for 3 days
4

Confirm and release

Check everything arrived as expected, then confirm. Only then is the farmer paid.

  • Something wrong? Report it before you confirm
  • Your money stays held while we look into it
  • Rate the farm to help other shoppers

What your order status means

You'll get an SMS or email each time it changes.

Waiting for the farmThe farm has up to 24 hours to accept your order.
AcceptedThe farm is preparing your produce.
PackedA rider is on the way to collect it from the farm.
Out for deliveryYour order is on its way to you or the pickup point.
DeliveredCheck your items, then confirm to release payment.
Problem reportedYour money stays held while Farmkeke reviews it.
